Jibo, a social robot powered by GPT-4, guides children ages 7-11 through conversations about art to practice social-emotional learning.

Children showed more empathetic reasoning when discussing emotional art than neutral art, and Jibo's responses helped ease their discomfort after sharing vulnerable feelings. This work won the RSJ Pioneering Research Award at RO-MAN 2024.
