A game where middle school students practice ethical decision-making about AI through scenarios grounded in virtue ethics.

In a workshop with 22 students ages 13-14, students learned about virtue ethics, played the game, and co-designed new scenarios for future versions. Led with Daniella DiPaola (IDC 2026).
