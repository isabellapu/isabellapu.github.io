A series of interactive digital storybooks that teach children ages 6-9 how AI works. Kids follow Doodlebot, a robot who dreams of becoming an artist, through mini-games and hands-on creative AI activities.

In a study with 57 children, the storybook version led to significantly greater learning gains than the same activities without the story (CHI 2026). A follow-up on multiple pathways to early AI literacy will appear at EAAI 2027. This project was also my M.S. thesis.
