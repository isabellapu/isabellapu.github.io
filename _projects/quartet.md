For my senior thesis at Princeton, I co-designed the Beatbots, a quartet of percussion robots, with musicians.

Instead of imitating human players, the Beatbots lean into what robots do well (precise timing, randomness, and tight coordination) to make music that is hard for humans to play. Audience members can join in through a MIDI keyboard. Published at HRI 2025.
