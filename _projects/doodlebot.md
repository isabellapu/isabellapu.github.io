Doodlebot is an educational robot from the Personal Robots Group that children program with blocks to draw, dance, express emotions, and use AI.

With Doodlebot, we studied how children ages 6-9 think robots should fit into everyday authority. Children resisted robot control in areas tied to their own skills and self-expression, and expected teacher, classmate, and mentee robots to behave differently (HRI 2026). We also found that changing a robot's voice, decorations, or costume shapes the traits and classroom roles children give it, but not whether they would listen to it (RO-MAN 2026).
