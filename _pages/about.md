Hi y'all! I'm **Isabella Pu**, a second year PhD student in the [Personal Robots Group](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/personal-robots/overview/) at the **[MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/)**, studying with [Professor Cynthia Breazeal](https://cynthiabreazeal.media.mit.edu/). In 2023, I graduated _magna cum laude_ from [Princeton University](https://www.princeton.edu/) with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and certificate in Robotics & Intelligent Systems, and in 2025 I received my Master's degree in Media Arts and Sciences from the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/). Previously, I am grateful to have been supported by the [MIT Presidential Graduate Fellowship](https://oge.mit.edu/fellowships/presidential-graduate-fellowship-program/).

My research examines how **social robots and AI** can help children **create, learn, and connect** with more **joy, confidence, and agency**, using methods grounded in **creative activity**. I pursue this through three main lines:

- **I build creative robot and AI systems** that scaffold children's art-making across the arts, helping them create with confidence, connect with others, and express their feelings.
- **I design creative learning experiences**, such as games and interactive storybooks, that help children learn through creating. I have a special focus on AI and robot literacy, so children canbetter decide for themselves whether and how to use these technologies.
- **I examine children's experiences with robots and AI**, studying how they perceive, trust, and relate to these technologies, to inform the design of technologies that better reflect children's perspectives.

In my research, I co-design with children; build systems, including full-stack web apps, multimodal generative AI pipelines, and expressive robot interactions; and evaluate them in ecologically valid settings, such as classrooms, museums, and homes.

Previously, I have been a Research Intern at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/) on the [Extended Perception, Interaction and Cognition (EPIC) team](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/epic/) and a Software Engineering Intern at [Blizzard Entertainment](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/) _(for the Horde!)_ on the [Player Interactions & Trust team](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/blizzard/23923815/coming-together-to-create-a-playbook-for-prosocial-game-design).

Outside of work, I am an avid achievement hunter and play all sorts of video games. I also follow fashion, write & play music, and write & perform poetry.
